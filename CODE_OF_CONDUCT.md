# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This repository exists to make completion claims more checkable. Discussion is
welcome when it is specific, evidence-led, and directed at the claim or change—not
the person making it.

Expected conduct includes:

- challenge ideas with a reproducible case, a resolvable source, or a clear argument;
- state uncertainty and limits plainly;
- make room for correction, translation, and good-faith disagreement;
- protect private information and respect attribution.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate misrepresentation, publication of
private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For an ordinary disagreement, use the objection issue form. For conduct concerns,
contact the maintainer privately at `NGHTBOY@sshlg.me`; do not open a public issue.
Reports are reviewed in confidence. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions,
limit participation, or ban an account when necessary to protect the project and its
participants.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is acting as
a representative of the project.
